# Code of Conduct

CoFi aims to be a professional, technically rigorous, and welcoming open-source project.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence;
- keep reviews specific, actionable, and proportionate;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, and security boundaries;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and disruptive behavior;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest when they could affect technical decisions.

## Technical disagreements

Resolve disagreements with reproducible examples, tests, specifications, and documented trade-offs. Financial correctness and repository invariants take precedence over preference or seniority.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior materially harms contributors or the project.

Serious or sensitive conduct concerns should be reported privately to the repository maintainer.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces controlled by CoFi maintainers.
